# About Sumanth

Sumanth is a software engineer with a full-stack and infrastructure background,
increasingly focused on applying AI to real products rather than demos. He likes
systems that are simple on purpose: the smallest architecture that is still
correct, observable, and easy to hand off.

This portfolio is itself an example of that approach - a Retrieval-Augmented
chat experience backed by structured, version-controlled data (this repository)
rather than a model fine-tuned on guesses.
